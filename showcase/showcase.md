# Refract

A quiet optical bench. A lattice of shapes. A picture waiting to be drawn in light. Refract asks you to trace a continuous beam between each pair of matching emitters, passing through every lens along the way.

Triangles glow mint, squares glow amber, and diamonds glow violet. Follow the shapes from node to node, diagonals included. Neutral crystals bring the beams together: every charge must be spent, and every crossing must be finished. Segments cannot overlap or cross, so making room for one beam can change everything about another.

Take your time. Release a beam to leave it in place, pick up either end to keep drawing, or backtrack to unwind your last thought. There is no clock to beat. The answer is the complete picture of light you leave on the bench.

Campaign offers 24 handcrafted studies, growing from a single channel to intricate arrangements of shared crystals. Cascade offers an endless succession of varied boards across five difficulty tiers. Choose a course, or settle into the flow.
